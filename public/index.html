How a drive works

Five questions: two team+position, two team+number, one multi-team. Each answer is a play — rarer answers gain more yards: rare +13 · uncommon +10 · common +6 · miss +0. Perfect play moves you ~65 yards, well inside the 20. Then you kick: the closer you got, the shorter the kick and the better your odds. Miss a couple and you're staring at a 55-yarder that probably won't make it.
